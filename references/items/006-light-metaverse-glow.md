## Visual analysis

- **Palette:** near-black base (approx. `#0A0A0A`); hero glow gradient from cream/ivory center (approx. `#F6E7CE`) through amber/orange (approx. `#E89B4A`–`#D97A2B`) fading into black; champagne-gold 3D objects (approx. `#E4C787`); saturated token-icon accents (pink, ethereum-blue/purple, green, yellow, orange — the only multicolor elements). Text: white on black sections, near-black on the glow.
- **Typography:** single bold geometric/grotesque sans throughout (Inter/SF Pro/Söhne family feel); headlines extra-bold with tight tracking, very large relative to the 370 px viewport; body small, grey/low-contrast; eyebrows are tiny letterspaced uppercase pills or plain caps. Strict hierarchy: one H1/H2, one sub-line, one CTA per section.
- **Composition & grid:** single centered column (mobile stacking); sections alternate centered (glow moments) and split text/graphic layouts, with the split flipping sides between sections for rhythm. Generous vertical spacing; long stretches of pure black separate the lit moments, so the glow reads as an event, not wallpaper.
- **Graphics:** the radial glow is the brand motif, echoed by eclipse spheres (bright crescent rim on dark ball) used as decorative planets and as avatars in the social chips. Everything else is floating collage: glossy token icons, asset tiles, 3D gold primitives, one dark product UI card. Orbit-line arcs add a thin-line counterpoint to the soft glow.
- **Motion:** static screenshot — none visible. Reasonably inferable: floating icons and orbit arcs suggest slow parallax/drift, and the glow could pulse on scroll, but this is speculation from composition only.

## Signature techniques

- **One glow, whole page** — a single warm radial light on near-black is the entire visual identity; reused at hero and closing CTA as bookends, so the page opens and ends on the same "sunrise".
- **Eclipse/planet motif system** — the crescent-rim sphere appears at every scale: giant decorative planet under the hero, floating orbs, avatar chips, protocol card illustration.
- **Inverted text polarity on the glow** — headlines turn black where the background is lit, white where it is dark; the light itself drives the type color.
- **Floating collage of colorful tokens** as product evidence (the product is the icons it aggregates).
- **Text/UI alternation rhythm** — split sections flip left/right down the page, keeping a one-column mobile scroll from feeling flat.

## Takeaways for Inside

- **The one principle to take:** one restrained light source as a recurring atmospheric anchor — a single warm radial glow on a near-black page, repeated at the hero and the final CTA. For Inside it could visualize "inside/behind the scenes" (light in a dark room, a working screen at night) without any illustration budget, fits the engineering mood (one controlled element, not decoration spray), and gives an authorial landing a memorable signature while keeping content dense and text-first. Hypothesis, not decision.
- **What must not be copied:** web3/hype vocabulary ("metaverse", "Web 3.0", "new era"), the NFT/token icon collage, glossy 3D gold primitives, and the Yoda quote — all off-character (hype-driven, borrowed personality, corporate-meme tone). Also not the low-contrast grey body text: our audience reads for substance, legibility beats mood there.
