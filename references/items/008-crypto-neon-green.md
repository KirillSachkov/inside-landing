## Signature techniques

- **Inline highlight chips in headline copy** — lime squares, glyphs
  (⚡, #, ✳) and mini-pills embedded *inside* sentence flow, turning the
  headline into a composed artifact rather than plain text.
- **Single neon accent on pure black** — one lime color does all the
  work: chips, badges, icons, one logo circle. Maximum contrast, instant
  recognition.
- **Stats floating at hero edges** — numbers with tiny labels and hairline
  rules pinned to the left/right margins, framing the headline with
  evidence without a dedicated "numbers" section.
- **Numbered section labels** — `01` / `02` numerals paired with a white
  pill tag naming the section ("About ✳ Network", "Investors of
  ✳ Network"), giving the page a spec/document rhythm.
- **Ticker pill** — a marquee restyled as a rounded lime card inside a
  dark pill track with numbered badges.
- **One accented item in a logo row** — all investor circles dark, a
  single oversized lime circle breaks the pattern and pulls the eye.

## Takeaways for Inside

- **The one principle to take:** *inline accents inside statement copy.*
  Embedding small chips/glyphs/highlighted words directly in a headline
  ("Our team ▮ has been creating ⚡ … for [Network] 5 years") makes a
  plain sentence feel engineered and dense with meaning — close to how
  developers annotate specs and docs. Hypothesis: the same technique with
  our palette and engineering glyphs (e.g. `{ }`, `→`, commit-hash chips,
  ADR-style tags) could make Inside's statement headlines feel authored
  and technical without any illustration budget. The numbered section
  labels (`01 About`) are a second, cheaper borrow: they read as document
  structure, which fits the engineering character.
- **What must not be copied:** the crypto-hype register itself — neon-lime
  on pure black as the *whole* identity, the ticker/marquee (casino
  energy), inflated edge stats (`93m+`, `3.2b`) we don't have, investor
  logo rows (Inside has no investors), and the decorative 3D props /
  "Scam detected!" gamification. Inside's proof must be real artifacts,
  not big numbers.
- **License constraints:** the grotesque is unidentified — do not assume
  it is free; pick a licensed/open equivalent (Inter, etc.) if imitating.
  The investor logos include third-party trademarks (e.g. a
  Binance-like diamond glyph) — never reuse. The asterisk brand mark and
  all copy are brand-specific; treat layout techniques only as reference.
