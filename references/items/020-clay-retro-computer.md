## What this is

A cute clay-render-style 3D illustration of a retro desktop computer: chunky
CRT monitor with rounded beige plastic body, dark screen showing two glowing
green oval "eyes", sitting on a system-unit base with a floppy slot; a small
potted plant and a wired mouse beside it. Solid lavender background, soft
studio lighting.

## Structure

Not a landing — a single-object scene. Relevant as the "object on a pedestal"
pattern: one characterful device, centered, few props.

## Visual analysis

- **Palette:** beige/cream plastic (`#E8E4D8`-ish), lavender bg (`#B9B9E0`-ish),
  green screen glow (`#39FF14`-ish), tiny red accent on the floppy slot.
- **Typography:** none.
- **Composition & grid:** single subject, 3/4 view, generous negative space.
- **Graphics:** soft clay/dough 3D — big radii, matte materials, soft shadows,
  subtle bevels; "face" via two glowing screen bars = instant character without
  a mascot costume.
- **Motion:** static image; implied animation potential — blinking eyes,
  screen flicker, gentle bobbing.

## Signature techniques

- Device-as-character through the screen alone (glowing eyes/bars), body stays
  a believable retro machine.
- Clay softness: every corner heavily rounded, no hard edges anywhere.

## Takeaways for Inside

- **The one principle to take:** a single tactile device object as the hero's
  right-hand visual — warmer and more ownable than abstract 3D shapes, and the
  screen can carry *our* content (terminal prompt, "S_", log lines) instead of
  a face. Hypothesis: clay-render retro computer with a live-looking terminal
  screen fits A2's soft spec-sheet and the "engineering, alive" character.
- **What must not be copied:** the "cute eyes" face — tips into mascot
  territory (off-character); lavender playground palette — ours is paper +
  orange.
- **License constraints:** source unknown (likely stock/AI-generated
  illustration) — do not reuse the image itself; regenerate our own object.
